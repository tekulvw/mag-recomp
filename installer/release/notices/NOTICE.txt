MAG installer (mag-recomp)

This archive contains the mag-recomp installer: its own source and profiles, a frozen
Python launcher, and a source kit with mag-recomp's tooling, sources and profiles plus the
pinned ps3recomp checkouts (MIT, see LICENSE.ps3recomp in the kit). It contains no MAG
assets, firmware, decrypted game images, generated or lifted code, built launcher or engine,
player accounts or build caches. The installer builds MAG on your computer from your own
game files; nothing it builds may be redistributed.

The frozen launcher embeds CPython. Its version and source inventory are recorded in
PROVENANCE.json; the interpreter's license is copied to licenses/. PyInstaller 6.22.3
supplies the bootloader under GPL-2.0 with its distribution exception, with selected files
under Apache-2.0. Its license notices and those of the pinned packager dependencies
accompany this archive.

Official references:
https://docs.python.org/3/license.html
https://pyinstaller.org/en/v6.22.3/license.html
https://github.com/pyinstaller/pyinstaller/blob/v6.22.3/COPYING.txt

Build MAG downloads further tools (Python, CMake, Ninja, MinGit, Python wheels and, on
macOS, SDL2, glslang, SPIRV-Cross and FFmpeg sources) from their official origins at
pinned versions, under their own licenses. On Windows, Microsoft's Visual Studio Build
Tools are installed through Microsoft's own installer and license.
